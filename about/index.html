Frequently Asked Questions
Does ABC Projects provide immigration legal advice?

No. ABC Projects provides research-profile, scholarly visibility and evidence-documentation support. Immigration eligibility, legal strategy and petition preparation should be handled by qualified immigration counsel.

Can citations guarantee EB-1A or NIW approval?

No. Citation evidence may help document scholarly impact, but no individual metric guarantees an immigration outcome.

Can you review my existing Google Scholar profile?

Yes. We can review publication records, citations, author identity, consistency and broader research visibility.

How long does research-profile development take?

The timeframe depends on the existing research record, the scope of work and the type of documentation required.

Do you guarantee publication?

No. Publication decisions are made independently by journals, publishers and their editorial or peer-review processes.

Can you organize evidence for peer-review and judging activity?

Yes. Existing invitations, certificates, acknowledgements and other verifiable records can be organized into a clearer evidence record.

Can you help document original research contributions and their impact?

Yes. Existing research contributions can be reviewed together with citations, independent recognition, use and other supporting evidence.

Can you prepare citation and research-impact records?

Yes. We can organize citation history, research-impact information and related scholarly evidence using available verifiable records.

Do you work with researchers who already have an immigration attorney?

Yes. ABC Projects can support the research-profile and academic-evidence side while legal strategy remains with qualified immigration counsel.

Can research profiles across Google Scholar, ORCID and other platforms be reviewed for consistency?

Yes. Researcher identity, publication records and profile consistency can be reviewed across legitimate scholarly platforms.

Can you help organize expert reference or recommendation evidence?

Yes. Existing scholarly records, contribution summaries, citation evidence and factual supporting materials can be organized for legitimate expert reference or recommendation documentation. We do not fabricate opinions or letters on behalf of experts.

Does ABC Projects write or file immigration petitions?

No. ABC Projects does not prepare or file immigration petitions and does not provide legal representation.
