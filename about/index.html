Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Our Services & Support

Quick answers about research-profile development, publication support, research visibility, citation evidence and professional documentation.

01Can you review my existing Google Scholar profile?

Yes. We can review publication records, citation history, author identity, profile consistency and broader research visibility to identify gaps, inconsistencies and practical areas for improvement.

02How long does research-profile development take?

The timeframe depends on the existing research record, the scope of work and the type of documentation required. After an initial review, we can define the work more clearly and provide a more realistic project timeframe.

03Can ABC Projects help improve my citation profile and research impact?

Yes. Our team can review your scholarly profile, publications, citation patterns and impact indicators, then provide tailored support for profile consistency, research discoverability, scholarly visibility and legitimate, sustainable citation-impact development.

04Do you provide end-to-end publication support?

Yes. We provide manuscript review, content improvement, editing, journal selection, journal-specific formatting and submission preparation. Through our publishing network and associated journal platforms, we may also support suitable manuscripts through the publication process. Each manuscript remains subject to the scope, editorial requirements, review standards and publication policies of the selected journal.

05Can ABC Projects help me publish my manuscript?

Yes. We can support authors from initial manuscript assessment and improvement through editing, journal matching, formatting and publication coordination. Depending on the manuscript and subject area, suitable options may include independent journals or journals available through our associated publishing network. The manuscript must meet the applicable journal's scope and editorial standards.

06Can research profiles across Google Scholar, ORCID and other platforms be reviewed for consistency?

Yes. We can review researcher identity, publication records and profile consistency across legitimate scholarly platforms and help identify discrepancies, missing records and opportunities for clearer presentation.

07Can you organize evidence for peer-review and judging activity?

Yes. Existing invitations, certificates, acknowledgements, review records and other verifiable materials can be organized into a clearer evidence record for legitimate academic or professional use.

08Can you help document original research contributions and their impact?

Yes. Existing research contributions can be reviewed together with citations, independent recognition, documented use and other supporting evidence to present the contribution and its impact more clearly.

09Can you prepare citation and research-impact records?

Yes. We can organize citation history, research-impact information and related scholarly evidence using available and verifiable records, with an emphasis on clarity, consistency and traceability.

10Can you help organize expert reference or recommendation evidence?

Yes. Existing scholarly records, contribution summaries, citation evidence and factual supporting materials can be organized for legitimate expert reference or recommendation documentation. We do not fabricate opinions, credentials or statements on behalf of experts.

11Do you work with researchers who already have an immigration attorney?

Yes. ABC Projects can support the research-profile, scholarly visibility and academic-evidence side of the work while immigration eligibility, legal strategy and petition preparation remain with qualified immigration counsel.

12Does ABC Projects provide immigration legal advice?

No. ABC Projects provides research-profile, scholarly visibility and evidence-documentation support. Immigration eligibility, legal strategy and legal advice should be handled by qualified immigration counsel.

13Does ABC Projects write or file immigration petitions?

No. ABC Projects does not provide legal representation or file immigration petitions. Our role is limited to research-profile development, scholarly visibility and the organization of legitimate academic and professional evidence.

14 Can citations guarantee EB-1A, EB-1B, NIW or O-1 approval?

No individual citation count or scholarly metric can guarantee an immigration outcome. Citation evidence may help document scholarly impact or international recognition, but its relevance differs across pathways. Immigration decisions depend on the applicable legal criteria and the totality of the evidence.

15Do you guarantee a specific number of citations?

We do not promise a fixed number of citations because citations ultimately depend on independent scholarly use and recognition of published research. We can assess the existing citation profile and develop a tailored strategy focused on discoverability, scholarly visibility, profile consistency and sustainable citation growth through legitimate scholarly practices.
