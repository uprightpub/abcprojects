Immigration Profile Support

EB-1A Extraordinary Ability Research Profile Support

Structured support for developing scholarly and professional records and organizing clear, accurate and verifiable evidence that may be relevant to attorney-led EB-1A extraordinary ability preparation.

Core EB-1A Extraordinary Ability Profile Support

01

EB-1A Profile Evidence Assessment

Review publications, citations, judging activities, original contributions, recognition, memberships, editorial work and leadership records to identify profile strengths and evidence gaps.

02

Scholarly Authorship & Publication Evidence

Organize scholarly articles, authorship records, journal information, DOI details, publication history and related evidence of research activity.

03

Peer Review & Judging Evidence

Document legitimate reviewer invitations, completed reviews, editorial evaluation, conference judging and other verifiable assessment activities.

04

Original Contribution & Impact Evidence

Organize research contributions, frameworks, innovations, citation records, independent scholarly use and available evidence of significance within the field.

05

Recognition, Membership & Leadership Evidence

Document relevant independent recognition, qualifying professional memberships, editorial appointments and leading or critical professional responsibilities.

06

Evidence Record Organization

Arrange supporting records, verification sources, dates, organizations and achievement details in a clear structure for independent attorney review.

EB-1A Profile Support Outcome

Clients receive a clearer EB-1A scholarly and professional profile and an organized, verifiable evidence record for review by their independent immigration attorney. ABC Projects does not provide legal advice, determine immigration eligibility or prepare and file petitions.
