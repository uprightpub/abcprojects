Immigration Profile Support

EB-1B Outstanding Professor & Researcher Profile Support

Structured support for developing scholarly records and organizing accurate, consistent and verifiable evidence of research experience, international recognition and academic impact for attorney-led EB-1B preparation.

Core EB-1B Outstanding Professor & Researcher Support

01

EB-1B Profile Evidence Assessment

Review research experience, publications, citations, judging activities, original contributions, international recognition and available records to identify profile strengths and evidence gaps.

02

Teaching & Research Experience Documentation

Organize academic appointments, teaching and research experience, employment records, institutional responsibilities and available evidence of qualifying professional work.

03

Scholarly Authorship & Publication Evidence

Document scholarly books, journal articles, conference publications, authorship records, DOI details and the applicant’s sustained publication history.

04

Peer Review & Judging Evidence

Organize legitimate reviewer invitations, completed reviews, editorial evaluation, conference judging and other verifiable evidence of evaluating the work of others.

05

Original Contribution & Citation-Impact Evidence

Document original scientific or scholarly contributions, citation records, independent scholarly use and available evidence showing influence within the academic field.

06

International Recognition Evidence Portfolio

Organize relevant awards, qualifying memberships, published material about the applicant’s work, independent recognition and supporting records into a clear evidence portfolio.

EB-1B Profile Support Outcome

Clients receive a clearer EB-1B professor or researcher profile and an organized, factual evidence record for review by their prospective U.S. employer and independent immigration attorney. ABC Projects does not provide employer sponsorship, legal advice, determine eligibility or prepare and file immigration petitions.
