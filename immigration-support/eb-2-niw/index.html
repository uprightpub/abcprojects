Immigration Profile Support

EB-2 NIW Research Profile Support

Structured support for developing professional and scholarly records and organizing factual evidence relevant to attorney-led EB-2 National Interest Waiver preparation.

Core EB-2 NIW Profile & Evidence Support

01

NIW Profile Evidence Assessment

Review education, professional experience, publications, research impact, contributions, proposed work and available evidence to identify profile strengths and documentation gaps.

02

Proposed Endeavor Research Record

Organize factual information describing the proposed work, its research foundation, practical focus, intended activities and potential relevance within the professional field.

03

Education, Expertise & Experience Evidence

Document academic qualifications, specialized knowledge, employment history, research experience, professional skills and other relevant background records.

04

Research Merit & Broader Relevance Evidence

Organize publications, projects, applications, citation records and independent sources that help explain the importance and broader relevance of the proposed work.

05

Track Record & Capacity Evidence

Document prior achievements, research progress, professional roles, collaborations, available resources and relevant plans demonstrating capacity to advance the proposed work.

06

NIW Evidence Record Organization

Arrange supporting records, source references, dates, organizations and achievement details in a clear and consistent structure for independent attorney review.

EB-2 NIW Profile Support Outcome

Clients receive a clearer professional and research profile, a structured proposed-endeavor record and organized factual evidence for review by their independent immigration attorney. ABC Projects does not provide legal advice, determine eligibility or prepare and file immigration petitions.
