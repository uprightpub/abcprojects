Four Profile Pathways

Different Pathways. Different Evidence Priorities.

EB-1A, EB-1B, EB-2 NIW and O-1 are distinct immigration pathways. ABC Projects does not determine legal eligibility. Our role is to help clients develop and organize scholarly and professional records that may support attorney-led case preparation.

EB-1A

Extraordinary Ability Immigrant Profile

Evidence for a permanent-residence extraordinary-ability profile may include publications, judging activity, original contributions, memberships, independent recognition and leading or critical roles.

Research & Professional Evidence

Where Scholarly and Professional Evidence May Matter

Strong profile development begins with legitimate achievements and verifiable records. Different forms of evidence can help explain a researcher's expertise, recognition and professional standing.

01

Publications

Peer-reviewed articles, books, chapters, conference papers and other documented scholarly outputs.

02

Citations & Research Impact

Citation history, independent scholarly use and consistent research-impact documentation.

03

Peer Review & Judging

Reviewer invitations, completed reviews, judging activity and other verifiable evaluation of the work of others.

04

Original Contributions of Major Significance

Research frameworks, innovations, methodologies and other original scholarly or professional contributions, supported by evidence of significance and independent recognition.

05

Expert Reference & Recommendation Evidence

Scholarly records, contribution summaries, citation evidence and other factual materials that may support independent expert or recommendation letters.

06

Membership & Editorial Roles

Relevant professional memberships, editorial appointments, committee roles, research leadership and other documented professional responsibilities.
