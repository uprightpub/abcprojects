Immigration Profile Support

O-1 Extraordinary Ability Profile Support

Structured support for developing scholarly and professional records and organizing accurate, consistent and verifiable evidence that may be relevant to attorney-led O-1 extraordinary ability preparation.

Core O-1 Extraordinary Ability Profile Support

01

O-1 Profile Evidence Assessment

Review publications, recognition, judging activities, original contributions, memberships, professional roles and available evidence to identify profile strengths and evidence gaps.

02

Authorship & Publication Evidence

Organize scholarly articles, professional publications, authorship records, journal information, DOI details and related publication evidence.

03

Peer Review & Judging Evidence

Document legitimate reviewer invitations, completed reviews, editorial evaluation, conference judging and other verifiable evaluation activities.

04

Original Contribution & Impact Evidence

Organize research contributions, professional innovations, citation records, independent use and available evidence of significance within the applicant’s field.

05

Recognition & Critical Role Evidence

Document awards, relevant memberships, published material, editorial appointments, leadership responsibilities and leading or critical professional roles.

06

O-1 Evidence Record Organization

Arrange supporting documents, verification sources, dates, organizations and achievement details in a clear structure for independent attorney review.

O-1 Profile Support Outcome

Clients receive a clearer O-1 scholarly and professional profile and an organized, verifiable evidence record for review by their independent immigration attorney. ABC Projects does not provide legal advice, determine O-1 eligibility or prepare and file petitions.
