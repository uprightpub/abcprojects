Rising Citation Matrix

Sustainable Research Impact Develops Over Time

A credible research profile usually develops through sustained scholarly visibility and engagement. When publications remain discoverable, researcher identity is consistent, and scholarly work continues to reach relevant audiences, citation activity may develop progressively across successive years.

Profile Perspective

A gradual year-by-year citation pattern can present a more natural record of developing scholarly influence than an abrupt, unexplained citation spike. Consistency across publications, visibility, citations and independent scholarly use helps create a profile that is easier to understand and document.

Progressive Citation Growth

2022 Researcher information becomes consistent
2023 Research reaches wider scholarly audiences
2024 Scholarly interaction continues to develop
2025 Independent scholarly use accumulates
