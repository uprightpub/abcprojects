Privacy & Data Use

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 30, 2026

ABC Projects is an online research-profile and professional-evidence support service serving clients internationally, including clients in the United States and India. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit abcprojects.org, use the Free Research Profile Evaluation or contact us.

1. Information You Provide

You may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, WhatsApp number, country, education, professional experience, publications, citations, research profiles, professional evidence, service interests and information included in messages or documents you send to ABC Projects.

The Free Research Profile Evaluation processes the responses entered in your browser to calculate an instant result. Assessment responses may be temporarily retained in your browser's session storage so that the form can function during the current browser session. At present, automated online delivery may not be configured; if you choose to continue through WhatsApp or email, the information you send through that service is transmitted to the relevant provider and received by ABC Projects.

2. Information Collected Automatically

With your analytics-cookie consent, Google Analytics may collect information such as pages viewed, approximate location, device and browser information, traffic source, session activity, form interactions and selected website events. ABC Projects has configured Google Analytics not to send names, email addresses, WhatsApp numbers, assessment scores or submitted research-profile details as Analytics event parameters.

If analytics consent is denied, analytics storage remains denied. Google's tag may transmit limited cookieless measurement signals in accordance with Google Consent Mode, but Analytics cookies are not granted by ABC Projects.

3. How We Use Information

  • To provide profile evaluations, requested information and relevant services.
  • To respond to questions and communicate about requested support.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot and improve the website.
  • To understand website performance and visitor interests when consent is provided.
  • To maintain records, prevent misuse and comply with applicable obligations.

4. Cookies, Local Storage and Consent

The website uses browser storage to remember your analytics-cookie choice and may use session storage to preserve assessment responses during a browser session. Google Analytics cookies are optional and are enabled only when you select “Accept Analytics.” Advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalization remain denied through the website's Google Consent Mode configuration.

You can change your choice at any time by selecting “Cookie Settings” in the website footer. Rejecting analytics updates the consent state and attempts to remove ABC Projects Google Analytics cookies from your browser.

5. Service Providers and External Platforms

ABC Projects may use service providers and external platforms necessary to operate and communicate, including website hosting, Google Analytics, email services and WhatsApp. When you follow a link to an external platform, that provider processes information under its own terms and privacy policy. Information may be processed in countries other than the country where you live.

6. Data Sharing

ABC Projects does not sell personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where necessary to deliver or operate a requested service, when you direct us to share it, to protect rights or security, or when disclosure is required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

7. Retention and Security

Google Analytics event and user data retention is configured for 14 months. Other information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected, for service records, dispute resolution, security or applicable obligations. Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used, but no method of internet transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Your Choices and Requests

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may be able to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, object to or restrict certain processing, withdraw consent or ask questions about how information is handled. We may need to verify a request before acting on it. To submit a privacy request, email office@abc.us.org.

9. Children

ABC Projects services are intended for adults and professionals and are not directed to children under 18. Please do not submit a child's personal information through the website.

10. Professional and Immigration Information

Information and automated profile indicators provided through the website are for research-profile development and preliminary informational purposes. ABC Projects is not a law firm, does not provide immigration legal advice and does not create an attorney-client relationship.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website practices, services or legal requirements change. The revised effective date will be displayed at the top of this page. Material changes may also be communicated through an appropriate website notice.
