Before You Begin

Review the Profile as One Connected Scholarly Record

A strong research profile is not limited to citation numbers or a single academic platform. It depends on consistent researcher identity, accurate publication information, discoverable scholarly work and verifiable professional records.

Work through each section and mark the items that are complete. Items that remain unchecked can help identify practical priorities for future profile improvement.

Checklist 01

Researcher Identity & Basic Information

Consistent identity information helps scholarly databases, publishers and readers connect publications with the correct researcher.

☐
Consistent Author Name The same primary name format is used across publications and profiles.
☐
Current Affiliation Institutional and professional affiliations are accurate and current.
☐
Professional Email A reliable professional or institutional email address is available.
☐
Research Areas Primary research interests and areas of expertise are clearly stated.
☐
Complete Publication List Journal articles, books, chapters and proceedings are recorded accurately.
☐
Accurate Bibliographic Details Titles, author names, journal details, volume, issue and pages are correct.
☐
DOIs and Persistent Links Available DOI records and stable publisher links are included.
☐
Authorship Consistency Author order and name spelling match the official published versions.
Checklist 02

Publication Records

Clear and accurate publication records improve verification, discoverability and consistency across scholarly platforms.

Checklist 03

Scholarly Profiles & Persistent Identity

Each scholarly platform serves a different purpose. Profiles should complement one another while presenting consistent information.

☐
Google Scholar The profile is public, accurate and free from unrelated publications.
☐
ORCID iD The ORCID record includes current identity, affiliation and research outputs.
☐
Scopus and Web of Science Author profiles are reviewed for duplicates or missing publications where applicable.
☐
ResearchGate or Institutional Profile Relevant professional profiles contain accurate and current information.
☐
Citation Record Accuracy Duplicate, unrelated or incorrectly attributed citation records are reviewed.
☐
Impact Indicators Citation totals and h-index values are recorded with the platform and date.
☐
Independent Scholarly Use Examples of meaningful citation, discussion or application are identified.
☐
Responsible Interpretation Metrics are considered together with field, career stage and publication history.
Checklist 04

Citations & Research Impact

Citation metrics become more useful when they are accurate, appropriately contextualized and supported by clear records of independent scholarly use.

Checklist 05

Peer Review, Editorial & Leadership Records

Professional activity becomes easier to verify when appointment, invitation and completion records are preserved systematically.

☐
Reviewer Invitations Legitimate reviewer invitations and related correspondence are preserved.
☐
Completed Review Evidence Certificates, acknowledgements or verified platform records are retained.
☐
Editorial Appointments Appointment letters, journal mastheads and official listings are documented.
☐
Academic Leadership Leadership responsibilities and institutional contributions are supported by records.
☐
Search Visibility A name search returns accurate and professionally relevant information.
☐
Personal or Institutional Page A stable page presents the researcher’s work, expertise and profile links.
☐
Professional Network Presence LinkedIn and relevant academic networks contain consistent information.
☐
Post-Publication Communication Legitimate research outputs are shared responsibly with relevant audiences.
Checklist 06

Digital Visibility & Scholarly Communication

Responsible scholarly visibility helps relevant audiences find, understand and verify a researcher’s legitimate work.

Checklist 07

Ongoing Profile Maintenance

Research profiles should be reviewed periodically rather than updated only when a formal application or professional need arises.

01

Review Quarterly

Check core profiles for new publications, incorrect records, affiliation changes and outdated information.

02

Preserve Evidence

Maintain organized copies of appointment letters, certificates, invitations, acknowledgements and significant professional records.

03

Record Changes

Keep dated records of important profile updates, citation metrics, new roles and scholarly achievements.

Responsible Use

Accuracy Comes Before Visibility

This checklist is intended to support accurate profile organization, responsible scholarly visibility and clear professional documentation. It should not be used to create, exaggerate or misrepresent academic achievements, citations, professional roles or research impact.

Important: This resource provides general educational guidance. It does not guarantee publications, citations, research outcomes, professional recognition or immigration outcomes, and it does not constitute legal advice.
