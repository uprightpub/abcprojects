Service 08

Original Contribution Documentation

Structured support for identifying and documenting research contributions, frameworks, innovations, scholarly influence and independent recognition of significant work.

Core Original Contribution Support

01

Contribution Identification & Assessment

Review publications, research projects, professional work and available evidence to identify original and potentially significant contributions.

02

Framework & Methodology Documentation

Explain and organize evidence relating to original research frameworks, models, methods, analytical approaches and practical applications.

03

Innovation & Technical Contribution Evidence

Document inventions, systems, designs, technical solutions, processes and other research-based or professional innovations.

04

Scholarly Influence & Citation Evidence

Organize citation records, citing publications, independent scholarly use and available evidence showing how others have engaged with the contribution.

05

Independent Recognition & Use

Collect third-party references, institutional adoption, professional use, expert recognition and other independent evidence relating to the contribution.

06

Contribution Evidence Portfolio

Prepare a structured record connecting each contribution with its supporting publications, impact evidence, independent recognition and verifiable source documents.

Original Contribution Evidence Outcome

Clients receive a clear and organized evidence record explaining what the contribution is, how it was developed, where it has been used and what independent evidence supports its significance.
