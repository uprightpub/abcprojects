Service 04

Peer Review & Judging Evidence

Structured support for developing legitimate peer-review activities and organizing clear, consistent and verifiable evidence of evaluating the work of others.

Core Peer Review & Judging Support

01

Reviewer Profile Assessment

Review subject expertise, publication history, research experience and existing reviewer activities to identify suitable peer-review areas.

02

Reviewer Profile & Application Support

Prepare reviewer information, areas of expertise, academic background and professional materials for legitimate journal and conference reviewer applications.

03

Peer Review Process Guidance

Guidance on review criteria, manuscript evaluation, constructive comments, confidentiality and responsible completion of assigned peer-review work.

04

Invitation & Completion Records

Organize reviewer invitations, acceptance records, completed-review confirmations, certificates and available verification documents.

05

Editorial & Judging Activity Documentation

Document editorial review, conference evaluation, competition judging, panel assessment and other legitimate activities involving evaluation of others’ work.

06

Peer Review Evidence Portfolio

Prepare a structured record showing journal or organization, review date, subject area, activity type and available independent verification for each completed activity.

Peer Review Evidence Outcome

Clients receive an organized and verifiable record of legitimate reviewer, editorial and judging activities, supported by available invitations, confirmations, certificates and completion records.
