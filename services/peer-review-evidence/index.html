Service 04

Peer Review & Judging Evidence

Structured support for developing legitimate peer-review, editorial and judging activities and organizing clear, consistent and verifiable evidence of evaluating the scholarly or professional work of others.

Peer Review, Editorial & Judging Evidence Support

01

Reviewer Profile Assessment

Review subject expertise, publication history, research experience and existing reviewer activities to identify suitable peer-review areas and opportunities.

02

Reviewer Profile & Application Support

Prepare reviewer information, areas of expertise, academic background and professional materials for legitimate journal and conference reviewer applications.

03

Academic Peer Review Process Guidance

Guidance on review criteria, manuscript evaluation, constructive reviewer comments, confidentiality and responsible completion of assigned peer-review work.

04

Reviewer Invitation & Completion Records

Organize reviewer invitations, acceptance records, completed-review confirmations, certificates and available independent verification documents.

05

Editorial & Judging Activity Documentation

Document editorial review, conference evaluation, competition judging, panel assessment and other legitimate activities involving evaluation of others’ work.

06

Peer Review Evidence Portfolio

Prepare a structured evidence record showing the journal or organization, review date, subject area, activity type and available independent verification for each completed peer-review, editorial or judging activity.

Peer Review Evidence Outcome

Clients receive an organized and verifiable record of legitimate reviewer, editorial and judging activities, supported by available invitations, confirmations, certificates and completion records.
