Service 01

Research Profile Assessment

A structured assessment of your publications, citations, scholarly profiles, professional activities and supporting evidence to identify existing strengths, evidence gaps and priority areas for further research-profile development.

What Our Research Profile Assessment Reviews

01

Publication Record Review

Review published articles, authorship records, publication details, DOI information and consistency across scholarly profiles.

02

Citation & Research Impact Review

Assess citation history, research-impact indicators and the available record of independent scholarly use and influence.

03

Google Scholar & ORCID Profile Review

Review Google Scholar, ORCID and other relevant academic profiles for accurate authorship, complete publication records and identity consistency.

04

Peer Review & Editorial Activity Review

Assess reviewer, editorial and judging activities together with invitations, certificates and other available supporting records.

05

Contribution & Professional Recognition Review

Review research contributions, professional achievements, leadership roles and evidence of independent recognition.

06

Research Evidence Gap Identification

Identify missing, incomplete or inconsistent scholarly and professional records that may require further development or documentation.

What You Receive from the Assessment

You receive a clear summary of your existing research-profile strengths, identified evidence gaps and the priority areas for further scholarly, visibility and professional-evidence development.
