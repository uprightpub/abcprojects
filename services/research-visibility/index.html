Service 03

Research Visibility & Google Scholar

Professional support for improving scholarly profile accuracy, research-platform presence, academic identity consistency and documentation of legitimate research visibility and impact.

Core Research Visibility Support

01

Google Scholar Profile Setup & Optimization

Create, organize, update and optimize a professional Google Scholar profile with accurate researcher, affiliation, subject-area and publication information.

02

Publication & Researcher Record Correction

Review attributed publications, remove incorrect records, merge duplicate entries and improve consistency of researcher name, affiliation and available publication details.

03

Research Platform Profile Development

Create, update and optimize researcher profiles, research backgrounds, subject expertise and publication records across ResearchGate , ORCID , Academia.edu , Kudos , Impactio and PublicationsList.org .

04

Professional & Research Portfolio Development

Develop and update structured professional and research portfolios presenting research background, publications, projects, expertise and achievements through LinkedIn and Google Sites .

05

Citation Record Review & Monitoring

Review citation history, identify attribution problems and monitor year-by-year citations, research-impact indicators and available citation records.

06

Research Visibility & Impact Documentation

Organize cross-platform research information and prepare clear records of research discoverability, citation impact, highly cited publications and independent scholarly use.

Research Visibility Support Outcome

Clients receive more accurate and consistent scholarly profiles, better-organized publication and citation records, stronger cross-platform research presentation and clearer documentation of legitimate research visibility and impact.
